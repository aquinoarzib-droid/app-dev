# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## About
**Stranger Things** is a *sci-fi horror* series set in the 1980s in the small town of Hawkins, Indiana. It follows a group of kids who uncover supernatural mysteries and secret experiments.

## Main Characters
- Eleven
- Mike Wheeler
- Dustin Henderson
- Lucas Sinclair
- Will Byers

## Why I Like It
1. The story is suspenseful and full of mystery.
2. The 80s music and setting are great.
3. The characters grow with every season.
